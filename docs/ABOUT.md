## 🎯 The Vision & Mission

SPrav™ Job AI was built to solve the fundamental friction in technical job hunting:
1. **Zero Fake Claims / Zero Hallucination:** Every cover note and STAR story is strictly anchored against your verified master resume ground truth.
2. **12-Channel Direct Career Discovery:** 100% direct corporate application URLs across Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Himalayas, RemoteOK, Remotive, Arbeitnow, Jobicy, WeWorkRemotely, and Recruitee.
3. **100% Air-Gapped Local Privacy:** All candidate resumes, job logs, and credentials stay entirely on your local PC in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SPravJobAI`. Zero telemetry or personal data is ever transmitted to external servers.
4. **₹0 Free Freeware:** Zero subscriptions, zero paywalls, zero hidden credits. Operates locally with free Cloud AI keys (Google Gemini 2.0 Flash / Groq) or 100% offline GPU inference with Local Ollama (Qwen 2.5 Coder).

## ⚙️ Architectural Highlights (v2.4.5 Pro)

- **Continuous Discovery Engine:** Asynchronous scrapers monitoring 28,700+ verified corporate job boards with sub-second deterministic SHA-1 deduplication.
- **Semantic ATS Matcher:** Vector cosine similarity running at 26,062 jobs/second with 6-pillar dynamic skill classification.
- **Guided 1-Click Dispatch:** 3-tier triage (🌟 Best Matches, 💜 Solid Near-Miss, 📋 Reviewable) for candidate review and immediate application.
- **FastAPI Core:** Python async backend delivering 57.1ms median latency and 154.4 requests/second throughput with SQLite WAL mode.
- **Windows Portable Standalone:** Single-click zero-install runtime packaging Python 3.13, React 19, and all dependencies in a lightweight 367 MB ZIP.
