# 🤝 Contributing & Community Guidelines for SPrav™ Job AI

Thank you for your interest in **SPrav™ Job AI**! While the core binaries are distributed as freeware, community contributions, feedback, bug reports, and documentation enhancements are warmly welcomed.

---

## 💡 How You Can Help

1. **Submit Bug Reports & Edge Cases**:
   * Found an ATS career portal that fails to parse properly?
   * Encountered an unexpected UI rendering bug on a specific screen resolution?
   * Open an Issue on GitHub with reproduction steps and logs.

2. **Suggest New Career Portals & ATS Integrations**:
   * We continuously expand our 1st-party scraper directory. If you know of high-density career boards (e.g. specialized EU, India, or US startup boards), suggest them in our Discussions!

3. **Improve Documentation & Examples**:
   * Submit pull requests for guides, resume optimization templates, or developer scripts in `examples/`.
