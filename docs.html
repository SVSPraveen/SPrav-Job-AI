📖 User Guide & Quick Start

Welcome to SPrav™ Job AI. Follow these instructions to launch your autonomous job search in under 60 seconds.

1. Installation & Running

  1. Download SPrav_Job_AI_Pro_v2.4_Portable.zip from the official Google Drive link.
  2. Extract the ZIP archive to any folder on your Windows PC (e.g. C:\Apps\SPravJobAI).
  3. Double-click SPrav Job AI.exe to launch the Command Center.

2. Uploading Your Master Resume

In the Dashboard or Knowledge Base, upload your master resume in PDF format. SPrav automatically parses your work experience, projects, skills, and metrics to form your personal ground truth.

3. Configuring Free Cloud AI or Local Ollama

SPrav supports 100% free operation using either:

  • Free Cloud AI: Get a free API key from Google AI Studio (Gemini 2.0 Flash) or Groq LPU Console. Paste it into Settings.
  • 100% Local Air-Gapped AI: Install Ollama on your PC and run ollama run qwen2.5-coder:7b. SPrav routes all scoring locally on your GPU with zero internet calls.

4. Setting Your Application Scope

Navigate to Application Scope to specify target job roles (e.g. AI Engineer, Generative AI, Full Stack Developer) and preferred locations (e.g. Remote, India, Bengaluru, San Francisco) using the 1-click quick-add palettes.

5. Guided 1-Click Dispatch

Open Action Required to review pre-screened, high-match opportunities ($\text{ATS} \ge 65\%$). For each job, inspect the tailored cover note, STAR behavioral alignment, and click Open & Apply to dispatch with 1 click!
